Solos: your child in the spotlight

Any child who wants to sing or play an instrument can have a solo spot at the party. Solos take about 20–25 minutes of the evening, so we plan the programme early.

  1. 1Tell us when you registerWrite what your child would like to perform.

  3. 3Solo showcase, 22 NovEach soloist performs at the Sunday rehearsal, and we set the concert running order.
