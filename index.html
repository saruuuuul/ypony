The songs

One Mongolian song, one English song, and a family song to close the evening. The kids start it and the parents join in.

Solos

Open to any child who wants to sing or play. The 22 November showcase keeps total solo time to about 20–25 minutes, so every performer has the room's full attention.

Practice at home

After each Sunday we share practice recordings in the parents' group chat. Ten minutes a day at home makes the difference with only four rehearsals.

Older kids lead

Ages 11–13 act as section leaders, MCs and stage crew, so they have a real role alongside the younger singers.

Logistics and safety

Taken care of, every Sunday

Transport

Two drivers run pick-up and drop-off routes. Seats are limited, so tell us on the form, and we'll match families for carpools too. Car seats for younger children, and rides only with your signed consent.

Lunch

Provided every Sunday. Tell us about allergies and dietary needs below.

Supervision

A named adult is in charge each Sunday, with the instructor, assistants and host families. Children are signed in on arrival and released only to a parent or approved driver.

Photos and video

A professional video journal of the month and the concert, shared only with participating families. Nothing is posted publicly without your consent.
